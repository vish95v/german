Class 8 Science Check

1. What is photosynthesis? (5 marks)
Answer: Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make glucose from carbon dioxide and water using sunlight. Oxygen is released.

2. Name the gas released during photosynthesis. (2 marks)
Answer: Oxygen

3. Why is the ozone layer important to living things? (5 marks)
Answer: The ozone layer absorbs most of the Sun's harmful ultraviolet radiation, which protects people, animals, and plants.

4. Give one difference between a physical change and a chemical change. (3 marks)
Answer: A physical change does not create a new substance. A chemical change does create a new substance.
